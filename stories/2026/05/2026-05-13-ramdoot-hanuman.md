ENGLISH:

In the heart of the mythic tapestry, beneath the boughs of Ashok Vatika, there whispers a tale eternal. Hanuman, the devout monkey-god, embarks on a celestial mission as RamDoot — the messenger of Ram. Draped in the colors of dawn, he bounds across lands and seas to where Sita, the goddess in exile, sits cloaked in sorrow. With fervent love, he unveils Ram’s ring, a circular emblem glowing with the promise of return and reunion. His presence, a miracle clad in loyalty, speaks volumes to the imprisoned queen’s heart, igniting hope amidst her desolation.

This poignant scene, alive with emotion and transcendent with faith, epitomizes the strength of devotion that bridges worlds. It finds everlasting expression in Mithila art, where intricate patterns celebrate this divine encounter. Vibrant colors and delicate linework weave together to tell the story — not just of a meeting, but of a cosmic promise; a testament to courage, love, and unbreakable bonds woven into the cultural fabric of time.
