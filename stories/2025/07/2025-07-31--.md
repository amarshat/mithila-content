Amidst the golden sands where the sky meets the ocean's edge, the air was thick with anticipation as Lord Ram commanded the fervor of celestial might. Draped in regal valor, he stood with his brother Lakshman and the undaunted Vanar Sena, each heart beating in rhythm to his cause. The sea stretched vast and unyielding, a testament to nature's indifference to mankind's pleadings. For days, Ram entreated the Ocean God with reverent song and sacrifice, seeking a serene passage to Lanka, holding firm the noble torch of dharma.

Yet silence was the sea's relentless reply, a quiet refusal that stirred the tempest within Ram’s tranquil soul. Eyes ignited with divine resolve and bow drawn taut, he summoned the roaring wrath of the heavens. Dark clouds swirled in veneration of his celestial prowess, lightning danced upon the horizon as if illustrating the cosmic balance of might and love. Then, as nature itself trembled under the weight of his righteousness, arose the humbled Ocean God, clasping hands in the universal gesture of surrender and respect.

परंतु, समुद्र उसकी प्रार्थना को मौन में डुबोता गया, यह चुप्पी राम की स्थिर आत्मा में उथल-पुथल भर देती। दिव्य दृढ़ता से जलती आँखें, धनुष बंधा और आकाश की गूंजती गर्जना का आह्वान किया। मानो विधि की कथा गुम्शुदा हो, बादलों ने उनके प्रचंड प्रभामंडल को सजावट की। जब प्रकृति स्वयं उनके धर्म के भार तले कांपी, तब विनीत होकर समुद्र देवता बाहर निकले, और नम्रता में हाथ जोड़ एकात्मता की प्रार्थना की।
